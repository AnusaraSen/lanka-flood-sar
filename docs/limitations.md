# Limitations

> This project is for educational and portfolio purposes. Flood maps are model estimates with
> uncertainty and must not be used for emergency or safety decisions.

Known failure modes to document with examples as they are found:
- Urban areas and flooded vegetation
- Radar shadow and steep terrain
- Wind-roughened water and very flat terrain
- Scene-specific artefacts

Domain-shift results depend on which events are in the data; do not generalise beyond them.
Reference maps are themselves approximations.
