# Case study: May 2017 Sri Lanka floods

Main case study. May 2016 Cyclone Roanu is a stretch goal (a truly unseen event).

## Reference products to check (verify availability in Week 1)
- Copernicus EMS activation EMSR205 (Sentinel-1 delineation maps)
- Dartmouth Flood Observatory event 4474 (GIS flood extent files)
- IWMI / RADARSAT-2 flood map for the Gin Ganga and Galle area (29 May 2017)
- Published analysis of the Nilwala River basin flood

## Plan
- Hold this event out of training and report the domain-shift drop.
- Run the pipeline on one or two river basins and a few dates before, during and after the event.
- Compare with reference products (overlap metrics) and baselines, noting that reference maps are
  themselves approximations.
- Show maps with uncertainty and discuss where the model fails.
